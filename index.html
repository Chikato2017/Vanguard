Service Catalog 06 Disciplines
01

Commercial & Industrial Power Distribution

+

Design, procurement, and physical turnkey installation of medium-voltage substations, main switchboards (MSB), copper busway systems, and motor control centers (MCC). Engineered to handle high fault current levels with zero compromise on safety clearances and thermal dissipation.

  • 480V / 208V / 13.8kV Medium-Voltage Substation Builds
  • Main Service Switchboards up to 6,000A
  • Overhead Copper & Aluminum Busway Distribution
  • Motor Control Centers & Variable Frequency Drives (VFD)
02

Architectural & Kinetic Lighting Design

+

Seamless architectural lighting integration that respects shadow, material texture, and sightlines. From museum-grade 98+ CRI gallery optics to DMX512 kinetic exterior building facades and Lutron Quantum centralized multi-universe control.

  • DALI-2 & DMX512 Kinetic Lighting Programming
  • Concealed Architectural Linear Profiles & Coves
  • Museum & Gallery 98+ CRI Low-Glare Optics
  • Centralized Scene Presets & Daylight Harvesting
03

Critical Infrastructure & Emergency Generation

+

Zero-interruption power systems for hospitals, data centers, and advanced manufacturing. Redundant dual-feed automated transfer switches (ATS), static uninterruptible power supplies (UPS), and diesel/gas synchronous generator plants.

  • Tier III & IV Data Center Electrical Topology
  • Closed-Transition Dual ATS Synchronization
  • Megawatt Standby Generator Sets & Fuel Systems
  • Static Lithium-Ion & Flywheel UPS Deployment
04

Renewable Microgrids & BESS Storage

+

Commercial rooftop and canopy solar photovoltaic installations tightly coupled with utility-scale battery energy storage systems (BESS). Peak-shaving algorithms and islanding controls ensure cost reduction and grid independence.

  • Commercial Solar Photovoltaic Arrays (200 kW – 5 MW)
  • Utility & Commercial BESS Battery Integration
  • Autonomous Islanding Microgrid Controllers
  • DC Fast Charging (DCFC) Commercial Fleet Depots
05

Smart Building Automation & BMS Integration

+

Interfacing power systems directly with Building Management Systems (BMS) over native BACnet/IP and Modbus protocols. Granular branch-circuit power quality metering, automated load shedding, and occupancy-driven HVAC interlocks.

  • BACnet/IP & Modbus Metering Integration
  • Dynamic Demand-Response & Automated Load Shedding
  • High-Density Tenant Sub-Metering Solutions
  • Enterprise Energy Dashboards & Power Quality Analyzers
06

Testing, Commissioning & Arc Flash Compliance

+

Rigorous third-party standard verification before energization. Certified IEEE 1584 arc-flash hazard analysis, protective breaker trip coordination, FLIR infrared thermal imaging, and high-potential insulation resistance diagnostics.

  • NFPA 70E Arc Flash Hazard Studies & Labeling
  • Selective Protective Relay Coordination
  • FLIR Calibrated Infrared Thermography Reports
  • High-Potential (Hi-Pot) & Megohmmeter Testing

01 / CAPABILITY

100% In-House Master Electricians

We never subcontract core high-voltage terminations, switchgear assemblies, or life-safety wiring. Every field lead is a licensed Master Electrician with NFPA 70E high-voltage certification.

02 / VELOCITY

Pre-Fabricated Conduit Skids

By modeling conduits in 3D BIM and pre-assembling modular copper busway runs in our offsite fabrication facility, we cut onsite installation hours by 35% while eliminating spatial clashes.

03 / ACCOUNTABILITY

Lifetime As-Built Traceability

Upon project energization, clients receive calibrated FLIR thermal baselines, circuit schedules, high-resolution one-line CAD schematics, and an immutable digital as-built archive.
